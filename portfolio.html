About me

Currently open to medior roles in Data Science — Machine/Deep Learning or medior positions as Data Analyst. Fast learner, self-taught, and motivated to grow quickly in a team environment.

My first experience with data was a three-month internship at Puratos as a Process Improvement Engineer trainee, where I conducted data analysis to support Lean optimization of raw-material transport streams, contributing to annual savings of about €150,000.

I then completed a six-week BeCode program as a Data/AI Engineer trainee, strengthening my foundations for end-to-end data work.

I next managed equipment and associated data at CHU de Liège as a Biomedical Engineer: I administered the CMMS database, performed data analysis, cleaning and visualization, and built a Python data pipeline that automated monthly reports to help executives in their decision-making processes.

After this role, to deepen my expertise and switch to a career in data, I pursued intensive self-learning (on Datacamp, Coursera, Stanford Online) in Python, statistics, machine learning, and Azure, focusing on data exploration, feature engineering, model fitting/validation, and MLOps.

This reorientation work paid off: I passed the technical assessments at Troople, where I had the opportunity to develop AI solutions addressing real business problems on live projects.

A brief health-related period (2018–2021) limited my ability to drive and to work in large industrial environments; I kept on fighting and continued upskilling, and I am fully operational today.

My roles in a company

When you hire me, you get a package of different skills. From an employer's perspective, this means greater flexibility and adaptability; very useful when you need to fill multiple roles within a project or if an employee leaves due to unforeseen circumstances.

I can switch from one hat to another, or even stack hats, making sure the company stays competitive.
